# Webinar CTA Kit

> Drafted 28 Aug 2026 — there was no existing webinar deck or follow-up email
> to edit (checked the repo and connected Google Drive, both empty), so this
> is written from scratch to match the voice in `06-Motion-A-Call-Script.md`:
> direct, warm, no fluff, benefit-forward. Build your actual slide/email
> around this copy.
>
> Link to use everywhere below:
> **`https://app.vibelabsagency.com/get-started`**
> For a specific partner's attendees, add `?ref=<their-referral-code>` (find
> the code on their card in `/partners`) so referrals auto-attribute.

---

## Closing slide(s) for the webinar deck

**Slide 1 — the offer**

> ### Want a site like the ones I just showed you?
> **Get one built for your business — free to see, no obligation.**
>
> 📱 **app.vibelabsagency.com/get-started**
> *(or scan the QR code)*
>
> Takes 60 seconds. We'll reach out to show you a real, working site for
> your business — hosting, AI chat, and review automation included.

Generate a QR code pointing at the same URL and drop it on this slide —
during a live webinar most people will scan rather than type.

**Slide 2 — the partner ask** *(only if you're pitching partners in this
session too)*

> ### Know a business that needs this?
> **Refer them, get paid $100 when they sign up.**
>
> Talk to me after the session and I'll set you up with your own link.

Keep this as a live, spoken ask rather than a self-serve link for now —
there's no public partner-signup page yet, so this slide is what turns a
"yes" in the room into you adding them in `/partners` afterward.

## Follow-up email (send within 24 hours of the webinar)

**Subject:** Here's that link from today (+ your free site)

**Body:**

> Hey [First Name],
>
> Thanks for joining today — hope the [webinar topic] stuff was useful.
>
> A couple of things before you go:
>
> **If you want a site built for your business:**
> It's free to see, no obligation, and takes about a minute to request:
> 👉 **https://app.vibelabsagency.com/get-started**
>
> We'll reach out to show you a real, working version — hosting, AI chat
> widget, and review automation all included.
>
> **If you'd rather refer someone than build a site yourself:**
> Just reply to this email and I'll set you up with your own referral link —
> $100 for every business you send our way that signs up.
>
> Either way, reply if you have questions — I read every one of these myself.
>
> — Cassey
