Questions

Before you ask

Is the audit really free, or is it a sales call in disguise?

Really free, and you get the document whether or not you ever speak to us. We ask for one reply telling us whether it was useful. That is the whole deal. Our view is simple: if the report is good, you will want to talk. If it is not, we have not earned the conversation.

How is this different from the free SEO reports I already get emailed?

Those are automated grids of red and green with no explanation and no human involvement. Ours names the specific file slowing your site down, counts the fields in your form, quotes the contrast ratio of your body text, and tells you what each defect costs in customers. A person reviews every report before it goes out.

What is "AI search" and why does it have its own score?

A growing share of people now ask ChatGPT, Claude, or Perplexity for recommendations instead of scrolling Google. Those systems read structured data — machine-readable facts about your business, services, hours, and prices. Most local business websites publish none, which makes them invisible to that entire channel. It is currently the cheapest competitive advantage available, and it will not stay that way.

Do I have to hire you to fix what you find?

No. The report is written so any competent developer can act on it, and plenty of people take it to the developer they already have. We would rather you fix the problems with someone else than not fix them at all.

How long does a rebuild actually take?

Most local business sites take three to five weeks from approval to launch. The critical performance and AI search fixes usually ship in the first week, because those are the ones losing you money right now.

What if my website is genuinely fine?

Then the report will say so, and we will tell you where to spend your money instead. That happens perhaps one time in ten, and we would rather be the people who told you the truth.
